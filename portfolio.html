Game Projects

A selection of ongoing prototypes built through hands-on iteration in Godot and Unity.

I use these projects to work through gameplay systems, interface design, progression, balancing, debugging, and the less glamorous job of making many interconnected parts behave consistently.

01

Sail & Raid

Fantasy naval action RPG / strategy game

Godot 4 · Active prototype

Explore a sailing overworld, raid hostile islands, build a crew, collect resources, and develop a home port as your infamy grows.

Systems
Overworld sailing and patrol encounters; island raids and party combat; equipment and inventory; resource economy; home-port progression; persistent day/night cycle
Technical work
Region-aware visual profiles, save-file time persistence, low-resolution 3D scaling with native-resolution UI, atmospheric lighting and fog, procedural island landmarks
My focus
Gameplay and progression design, economy design, UI direction, encounter balance, visual feedback, testing, and debugging

02

8×8 Dungeon

Compact action roguelite

Godot 4 · Active prototype

Fight through increasingly dangerous procedural dungeon floors, assemble a three-weapon build, collect relics, and decide when to explore, shop, or push toward the next boss.

Systems
Procedural floors; minimap and fog of war; experience and three-choice upgrades; distinct weapon behaviors; relic events; loot, shops, bosses, and healing resources
Progression
Difficulty scales with both elapsed time and dungeon depth, while drop weighting, upgrade choices, and rising shop costs shape each run
My focus
Core-loop design, weapon tuning, difficulty pacing, readable combat feedback, interface cleanup, playtesting, and bug triage

03

Space Fighter Rhythm Shooter

On-rails shooter with timing-based weapons

Unity · Prototype

A third-person space-combat prototype that pairs continuous machine-gun fire with missiles launched through a rhythm-input system.

Systems
Mouse-following crosshair, target designation, missile lock states, machine-gun heat management, rhythm inputs, enemy waves, health, and crew callouts
Presentation
Variable flight paths, a futuristic combat HUD, anime-style crew portraits, readable weapon states, and layered audio feedback
My focus
Game concept, combat controls, rhythm mechanic, HUD iteration, weapon balance, visual direction, testing, and debugging

04

School Horror Prototype

Third-person narrative horror game

Godot 4 · Prototype

A story-focused horror prototype following Rose and a small group of students trapped inside a school where only they can perceive what is haunting it.

Systems
Third-person character movement and collision, spring-arm camera, interactive doors and scene loading, visual-novel dialogue, portraits, and emotional character states
Environment
A playable school-hallway graybox with lighting, rooms, navigation space, interaction points, and a foundation for story-driven exploration
My focus
Story concept, character direction, dialogue presentation, interaction flow, UI feedback, environment planning, and playtesting
