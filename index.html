About

I’m Alex, an IT professional and systems-minded builder based near Pittsburgh.

I’ve supported a K-12 school district, a 28-location furniture retailer, and field connectivity installations across Western Pennsylvania. Those environments taught me how to stay calm, isolate the real problem, and explain the fix without burying people in jargon.

I earned my B.S. in Information Technology – Cyber Security from the University of Pittsburgh at Greensburg, with a minor in Computer Science. My interests sit where support, infrastructure, and security overlap: endpoints, identity, Windows administration, networks, deployment, and the practical work of keeping systems reliable.

Outside of IT, I build game prototypes in Godot and Unity. That work exercises many of the same habits I bring to technical support—debugging, testing, documenting, and iterating until a system behaves the way it should.

Currently seeking: IT support, desktop support, field technology, NOC, or junior infrastructure opportunities in the Pittsburgh area.
